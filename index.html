Natascha Burk is a product designer based
in Kassel, Germany. Her practice combines
industrial design with experimental
approaches to form. Her work spans
self-initiated furniture and object projects
that investigate processes, technologies,
and materials in unconventional contexts,
as well as industrial design collaborations
with institutions and companies.
She has been teaching design since 2022
and currently works as an artistic associate
at Kunsthochschule Kassel. She has also
held lectureships at other universities and
institutions.
